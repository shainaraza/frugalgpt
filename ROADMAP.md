Fixes: 0/1 check on `correct`, reject empty query sets, integer-only `order`, `chain_length` stored in result. - **2026-11-07** — Step 6: `mpi.py` (MPI matrix, Eq. 2 top-m pruning; ties → lower index) and `optimizer.py` (exhaustive or MPI-pruned chains; official quantile parameterization on scores, 40-point grid + Nelder-Mead, penalty, non-decreasing quantiles, first-LLM-too-expensive skip, first chain wins ties). Fast grid: walks quantile prefixes and handles the last scored step on sorted scores; identical to point-wise evaluation and to a restatement of the official objective (tied scores included). HEADLINES with synthetic scores at budget = GPT-4 cost / 5: exhaustive 1320 chains in 11 s, pruned 108 chains in 0.9 s, same chain.
